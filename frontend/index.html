HOW IT WORKS

From “I’m stuck” to “I’ve got it”

Most platforms only track what was completed. SkillPulse helps tutors notice who is struggling, and why, before it is too late.

  1. Reflect

    After each class, learners mark it Green, Yellow or Red.

  2. Ask for help

    A learner reports a blocker in a few words.

  3. Support

    A tutor takes it, helps, and records what they did.

  4. Progress

    The blocker is resolved and readiness updates.

FAQ

Questions

How do I get an account?

SkillPulse is invite-only. Your tutor or academy admin invites you, and you receive an email with a temporary password. There is no public sign-up.

Which course is available?

The current course is AWS Solutions Architect Associate readiness, covering networking, VPC, storage and S3.

Who can see my progress?

You, the tutors of your course and the admins of your academy. Other learners cannot see your results.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. SkillPulse works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Is the readiness score a guarantee?

No. It is a guide to help you decide what to practise next, not a promise of exam success.
